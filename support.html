How do I unlock an app?

Open the locked app from your Home Screen and tap Earn time with reps. Tap the notification that appears to jump into a workout in FormLock. When you finish your reps, the app unlocks for the minutes you earned. Those minutes only count while you're actually using the app.

My reps aren't being counted

  • Stand your phone up on the floor about 2 metres away. Side-on works best; facing you works too.
  • Keep your head, shoulders and arms in view, in good light.
  • Go all the way down and all the way back up. Half reps and sagging hips don't count, on purpose.
  • During a workout, tap the exercise name at the top to see what the camera is tracking.

Why can't I change my settings right away?

To stop impulse changes, changing what's locked, the prices or the daily limit requires a request and a 1-hour wait. After the wait you have 15 minutes to make changes.

How do I stop FormLock or remove it?

In FormLock, open Settings (the gear icon), request a change, and after the wait tap Stop locking and reset. This removes every lock. You can then delete the app. You can also turn off FormLock's Screen Time access at any time in the iPhone Settings app under Screen Time.

Does FormLock record me?

No. The camera is only analysed live on your iPhone to count reps. Nothing is saved or uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.
